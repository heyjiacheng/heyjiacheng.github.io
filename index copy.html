About Me

I'm Jiahang Cao, an incoming PhD student at the University of Hong Kong. Previously, I obtained my bachelor's degree in Math at Sun Yat-sen University, and an MPhil degree at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). My research focuses on robot learning, generative models, and cognitive-inspired models.
